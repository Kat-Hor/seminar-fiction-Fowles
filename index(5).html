Ending I · Chapter 44Charles returns to Ernestina: the Victorian ending.

“There is only one good definition of God: the freedom that allows other freedoms to exist.” The French Lieutenant’s Woman, ch. 13 “Man is condemned to be free.” Jean-Paul Sartre, Existentialism Is a Humanism

Fowles lets his narrator step into the story, admit that he does not control his characters and, in the end, offer more than one ending. These pages collect the background you need for that game with freedom: the author’s life and work, the philosophical concepts behind it and the topics for your presentations.
